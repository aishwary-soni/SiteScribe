Chapter 4 of 9

WHAT THE AI DOES

Three engines. One shared brain.

↔

Language Bridge

Translates between owner, architect and contractor in their own language. Keeps one record of every conversation.

⌗

Project Brain

Connects plans, materials, workers, costs and dates into a living map. Makes the daily to-do list.

▤

Smart Buy

Finds nearby vendors, compares prices and suggests when to buy. Advice always comes with reasons.

THE CHANGE AGENT

When the owner says: “Make the bathroom bigger.”

  1. Owner speaks in Marathi.
  2. AI translates the clear request.
  3. It checks plan, materials and schedule.
  4. It works out extra cost and days.
  5. It finds vendors and the best time to buy.
  6. It rewrites the schedule in each language.
  7. It STOPS and asks a human to approve.
  8. After approval, it updates the shared record.

The AI does the work. Humans make the decisions.
