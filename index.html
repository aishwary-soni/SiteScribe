What the AI does

Three engines feed one shared brain. The AI handles the chain, but the step that matters most stays human.

EngineWhat the AI doesWhat the human does
Language BridgeTranslates conversations and keeps one record in the team's languages.The other person confirms costs and measurements.
Project BrainLinks plans, materials, workers, costs and dates into a schedule.Contractor checks the list and sends a site photo.
Smart BuyFinds nearby vendors, compares prices and gives reasons.Contractor chooses the vendor and places the order.

How the AI becomes a business

Free for the first month, then a monthly or yearly subscription. Contractors and freelance architects pay. Owners join free as invited guests. SiteScribe tracks wages owed but never moves money.

WhoPays?What they get
ContractorYesFewer surprise costs, approvals and a daily to-do list.
Freelance architectYesPlans explained in the client's language.
HomeownerFree, invitedSees the cost and time effect before saying yes.
Vendor / workerNoClear orders, delivery dates and daily tasks.
