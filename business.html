01 / VALUE PROPOSITION

For contractors

Protect profit and time.

See how a change affects materials, workers and dates before saying yes. Keep proof of approvals.

02 / STRONG MOAT

WHY THIS CAN LAST

The moat is the renovation memory layer.

A competitor can copy a chat screen. It cannot quickly copy years of trusted, multilingual project history that connects a firm's plans, prices, vendors, approvals, workers and outcomes.

MOAT 01

Embedded workflow

Start with the tools teams already use: WhatsApp voice notes, photos, bills and Excel. Less training means faster adoption.

MOAT 02

Proprietary project graph

Every approved change improves the map of dependencies, vendors, work and dates for that firm. The useful record gets richer over time.

MOAT 03

Trust across three sides

Owners, architects and contractors all see the same record. Leaving means losing shared context, not just switching software.

MOAT 04

Local language and outcome data

Indian language workflows, correction logs and real renovation outcomes make the service better in the market it serves.

Strong moat in one line: SiteScribe owns the trusted, multilingual record of how renovation decisions become real work.

04 / PORTER'S FIVE FORCES

THE BATTLEFIELD

Our biggest threats are Excel and small buyers.

The strategy is to work on top of existing habits, then make the shared record more valuable with every project.

01 · RIVALRY

Medium

Global construction platforms serve organised firms. SiteScribe serves the WhatsApp world and starts with language.

02 · SUBSTITUTES

Highest threat

Excel, WhatsApp and human site managers are familiar and cheap. We work with them instead of asking teams to start over.

03 · BUYER POWER

High

Small firms can leave easily. The free first month lowers risk, while project history and proof of approvals create useful switching cost.

04 · SUPPLIER POWER

Medium

AI model and language providers matter. We stay model-flexible and keep ownership of project data and correction history.

05 · NEW ENTRANTS

Medium

An app is easy to copy. Years of trusted outcomes, local language corrections and shared records are not.

05 / ABOUT SITESCRIBE

THE COMPANY

Built for the people between the blueprint and the brick.

SiteScribe is a fictional class-project company based in India. It is designed for renovation contractors, freelance architects, homeowners, vendors and workers who need one clear record.

Our mission is simple: no more surprises in renovation. Our vision is every renovation in India, in every language, on one shared record.

The product is an early concept. Claims about the moat, network effects and pilot measures are hypotheses to test, not made-up results.
